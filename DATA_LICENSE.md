# Data licences

## Data generated in this work
All numerical results, figures and tables produced by the code in this repository (`figure_code/data`,
`figure_code/figures`, `tables/*.json`, `tables/output`, `derivations/*.json`) are released under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Please cite the paper and this repository when using them.

## Third-party data
* `data/external/pib/PIB_freqsweep_flowcurve.xlsx`: frequency sweep and flow curve of a 2 wt% polyisobutylene
  solution, N. King, figshare (2026), https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.32005782, licensed CC BY 4.0 by its
  author and redistributed here unchanged. Original publication: N. King and G. H. McKinley,
  Phys. Rev. Lett. 137, 144003 (2026), https://doi.org/10.1103/hx2j-pt1k.
* `data/nemd/` and the values typed into `figure_code/fig6_nemd.py`: numerical values read off the published
  figures (digitized) and tables of P. J. Daivis, M. L. Matin and B. D. Todd, J. Non-Newtonian Fluid Mech. 111,
  1-18 (2003), https://doi.org/10.1016/S0377-0257(03)00011-9, and 147, 35-44 (2007),
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnnfm.2007.06.005. They are provided only so that the analysis can be reproduced;
  please cite the original papers. The published figures themselves are not included.
